The Method

DMAIC, applied to your business

Borrowed from Six Sigma, because "figure it out as you go" is exactly what got you stuck. Every engagement follows the same five stages.

01 Define We name the actual bottleneck — usually you — and define what "running without you" needs to look like for this specific business.
02 Measure Where does time actually go. Which decisions genuinely need you, and which ones only need you because no one else was ever allowed to make them.
03 Analyze Root cause, not symptoms. Jugad gets replaced with a diagnosis of why the same fires keep starting.
04 Improve Small, atomic changes to habits and process — built one at a time so they actually stick, not a 40-page SOP nobody reads.
05 Control The system is tested without you in the room. Sessions taper as it holds — twice a month becomes once a month.
