The Method

DMAIC, applied to your business architecture

Adapted from Six Sigma manufacturing. Because "improvising as you go" is exactly what keeps your calendar burning.

01 Define

Isolating the bottleneck

We map out the current structure and identify the core systems constraint. Usually, the constraint is you. We outline what "founder-free operations" looks like for your business model.

02 Measure

Tracking time & decision points

We log every decision, approval, and fire you personally handled over 14 days. We separate high-value leverage decisions from administrative tasks masquerading as urgency.

03 Analyze

Root-cause diagnosis

We replace temporary "jugaad" with systemic answers. If a fire repeats on the floor, we don't just put it out — we find out why the fire alarm system or standard operating procedure failed.

04 Improve

Atomic process redesign

We build lightweight, highly actionable SOPs your team can actually execute. We implement modern AI automated flows where human error can be engineered out of the loop.

05 Control

Autonomy stress-test

We test the business by stepping you out of meetings. First for 3 days, then 7, then 14. Sessions taper from twice-a-month to once-a-month as your dashboard holds steady without you.
